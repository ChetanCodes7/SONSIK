About SONSIK

Institutional History & Mission

Founded in 2004, the Society of Nepalese Students in Korea (SONSIK) is the non-profit, non-political student organization representing all Nepalese scholars, researchers, and academicians across South Korea.

We work to foster unity, academic excellence, career mentorship, and emergency health & welfare support through our regional networks and institutional partnerships.
