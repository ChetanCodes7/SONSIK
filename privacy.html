Privacy Policy

The Society of Nepalese Students in Korea (SONSIK) operates in full statutory compliance with the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) enforced by the Government of the Republic of Korea. As the primary official representative body for Nepalese scholars, researchers, and students across South Korean academic institutions, SONSIK strictly enforces data protection standards designed to safeguard user identity, academic records, and communication logs. To efficiently administer national memberships, organize academic symposiums, coordinate regional chapters, and provide institutional support, SONSIK collects minimal necessary personal details—including full name, university affiliation, degree program (Undergraduate, Masters, PhD, Postdoctoral, or Language Student), contact email, phone number, and Alien Registration Card (ARC) verification data. All general member information is collected on an annual registration cycle and is securely retained for a maximum duration of one (1) year (matching the active executive tenure and academic cycle). Upon the completion of this one-year period, or immediately upon a student's graduation and departure from South Korea, all stored personal records are permanently and systematically erased from our servers using non-recoverable digital destruction methods in accordance with Korean regulatory standards.


Above all, SONSIK upholds an uncompromised commitment to protecting the absolute confidentiality of every student member. We recognize that studying abroad in South Korea presents unique academic, cultural, health, and financial challenges, and we guarantee that every student's interactions with our platform remain completely private and secure. In particular, all documentation, medical records, financial proofs, and personal narratives submitted under the Emergency Student Care Fund (SCF) are classified as top-tier confidential assets. To protect student dignity and personal privacy during critical life emergencies, Emergency Student Care Fund (SCF) records are accessed exclusively by authorized committee officers who are specially appointed by the Executive Board solely for evaluating eligibility and processing emergency financial or medical relief. These sensitive welfare files are stored in isolated, high-security databases and are strictly barred from general executive committee viewing, public disclosure, or external inspection under any circumstances.
