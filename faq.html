Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for SONSIK membership?

All Nepalese undergraduate, graduate, PhD, postdoc, and language students studying at South Korean universities are eligible.

How is the Executive Committee selected?

The Executive Committee is elected at the Annual General Meeting, as defined in the SONSIK constitution. The current leadership is the 23rd Executive Committee.

Does SONSIK provide shcholorship?

SONSIK does not award scholarships directly but runs guidance seminars and shares verified information about GKS and university scholarships.

How do I apply for membership?

Fill in the membership application form on the Membership page, your application is reviewed by the Executive Committee.
