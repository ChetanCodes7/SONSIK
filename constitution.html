Constitution of the Society of Nepalese Students in Korea (SONSIK)

(With 11th Amendment, August 2025)

Preamble

We, the Nepalese students studying at different Academic Institutions in Republic of Korea, having felt the need to establish a unified students’ Society to promote our academic, professional and other mutual interests through wider, regular and frequent exchange of views among ourselves, and believing that closer relations with various academic institutions and professional groups in Korea, Nepal and abroad would be helpful in promoting our common academic interests, do hereby agree to form ourselves into a voluntary society and adopt this constitution.

Article I: Name, Objectives and Guiding Principles

A. Name, logo and Scope

1. The Society shall be known as the Society of Nepalese Students in Korea, or Nepal Bidhyarthi Samaj Korea (नेपाल विद्यार्थी समाज कोरिया), or Hanguk Nepal Yuhaksaenghoe (한국 네팔 유학 생회) in Korean, and may be referred to in short as SONSIK

2. The Logo of the Society shall be as shown in Annex I.

3. The official correspondence address of SONSIK shall be the address of the President or Secretary, as determined by the Executive Committee (EC).

4. The organization shall operate its activities in all provinces and territories of South Korea, and it can open its branches or local offices within South Korea, if necessary.

B. Terms and Abbreviations used in this constitution

The abbreviations and terms used in this constitution solely mean to explain the interest of student’s welfare; its objectives and working limits will be within the framework of the constitution approved by the General Assembly of this society. If anything else or otherwise is not taken, this Constitution will define the following terms accordingly:

1. “Students” mean Nepalese students enrolled for 6-months or more than 6- months’ course in any university, institute or school in South Korea.

2. “SONSIK” means the Society of Nepalese Students in Korea

3. “GA” means the General Assembly of the SONSIK.

4. “Executive Committee (EC)” means the elected governing body acting on the behalf of the SONSIK.

5. “President” means the executive President of the SONSIK.

6. “Vice-president” means the executive Vice- President of the SONSIK.

7. “Secretary” means the executive Secretary of the SONSIK

8. “Joint-secretary” means the executive Joint-secretary of the SONSIK.

9. “Treasurer” means the executive treasurer of the SONSIK.

110.“Executive Member” means the member of the Executive Committee of the SONSIK.

11.“SONSIK Member” means a member of this organization as described in Article-II. A of this constitution

12.“Fiscal Year” means the Period of 12 months starting from the first day after the Annual General Meeting ended.

13.“Society” means the Society of Nepalese Students in Korea.

C. Objectives

1. To facilitate and expose academic, cultural and social engagements among SONSIK members.

2. To develop network and communication among SONSIK members.

3. To provide forum for the presentation and promotion of innovative ideas among its members.

4. To act as a channel in strengthening relations among Nepal, Korea and other countries in the field of research, education, socio-economic, cultural and technological development.

5. To coordinate with various academic institutions as well as education related organizations including scholarship providers and policy framers.

D. Guiding Principle

The guiding principles of the society shall be as follows.

1. The Society shall act as a common forum for Nepalese students studying in South Korea to facilitate exchange of views and promotion of mutual cooperation.

2. The Society shall act as an umbrella body of all other regional/local association of Nepalese students at any Korean universities and work to activate students for strengthening their mutual cooperation.

3. The Society shall work for the promotion of academic, cultural, social and other common interests among its members.

4. The Society, through EC shall maintain close links with other non-political institutions of in Korea and other countries to promote Nepali culture and to exchange academic activities.

5. The Society shall help to get information regarding study opportunities in Korea to those Nepalese who are interested to study in South Korea. The help should be for non-business purposes.

6. To fulfil the above guidelines, the Society shall also keep in touch with other Nepalese academicians, researchers and professional groups living in Korea as and when necessary.

7. The Society shall refrain from any politically motivated activities.

Article II: Membership/Affiliations

A. Membership

1. Regular Membership: Regular membership shall be offered to all Nepalese individuals who have been enrolled as a student at Korean Universities in undergraduate, graduate, vocational, associate degree and Korean language courses at least for six- months.

2. Affiliated Membership: Affiliated Membership shall be offered to those Nepalese individuals, who have graduated from Korean universities and currently living or working in Korea, or to those Nepalese who haven’t graduated from Korean universities but currently involved in research or teaching activities in Korean academic institutions.

3. Honorary Membership: Honorary Membership shall be awarded to any person whom the Executive Committee feels appropriate to provide the membership on the grounds of the individual’s academic achievements and meritorious services to Nepal or Nepalese Community in Korea.

4. Junior Membership: All Nepalese students studying in Korea at high school standard or below that standard shall be the junior member of SONSIK.

B. ‘Friends of SONSIK’

‘Friends of SONSIK’ shall be awarded from the decision of Executive Committee to any Foreign or Nepalese individuals/organizations who can give or have given contribution for the SONSIK and are involved in educational or social development of any such projects in Korea or in Nepal.

C. Duties and Responsibilities of Members

1. It will be the prime responsibility of every member to abide by this constitution, the decisions of the Society and to support in achieving the goals of the Society to the member's fullest potentials.

2. Duty of a member shall be to contribute actively as much as possible in each activity to be conducted by the organization.

3. All the members shall have duty to contribute actively to the policy, program, and appeal of the organization.

4. All the members (except honorary members) shall update their membership status and other details every year.

D. Rights and Privileges of Members

1. Each Member will have individual rights and accountability which will not be transferable.

2. A Regular Member shall

(a) Be eligible to vote for and can be candidate for any Executive Committee post and/or other posts of the Society.

(b) Be entitled to receive all official publications of the Society.

(c) Have rights to participate in General Assembly. Each regular member shall have a right for one vote.

3. Affiliated Members, Honorary Members, Junior Members, Institutional Members, and Friends of SONSIK shall have right to participate in the AGM and can receive all official publications of the Society. However, they shall have no right to be a candidate for any post of the Executive Committee as well as to cast vote in any election of the Society.

E. Conditions to Cease Membership or Impeachment and Dismissals

The membership of a member shall not exist under the following conditions.

1. The membership will automatically be cancelled if the person no longer meets the qualifications for the membership.

2. If a member is found guilty of misusing SONSIK’s property, the EC can file a case against accused as per the law of South Korea or Nepal.

3. Any member of the organization may renounce from the membership through submitting resignation in writing after clearing outstanding dues.

4. Membership shall be cancelled if a member assists or participates in any subject matter that runs against the interest and objectives of the organization.

5. If the General Assembly passes resolution to remove any members by 2/3 majority of attendees.

6. If a member commits any prohibited act as specified by this constitution.

7. If a member fails to pay outstanding membership fee.

8. Executive Committee may suspend or remove the membership of SONSIK members in case he/she acts against the spirit of SONSIK and behaves objectionably with SONSIK members. However, every opportunity shall be afforded to such Member to clarify and defence against the accusations.

F. Regular Membership fee and Renewal

1. The membership fee for individual member shall be 10,000 (Ten Thousand) Korean Won, and annual renewal fee is 10,000 (Ten Thousand) Korean Won.

2. Regular membership shall be open throughout the year. Membership shall be effective for one year (12 Months) from the date of issuance.

3. Regular membership should be renewed yearly. Failure to renew the membership shall automatically be terminated.

4. Executive committee may modify the regular membership fee.

Article III: Provisions related to General Assembly, Executive Committee and Meetings

A. Composition of General Assembly

1. All the members mentioned in article II (A) of the constitution shall form the General Assembly. As the Assembly is the main decisive organ of the organization, affiliated members, honorary members, junior members, and advisors shall only be participant as invitee members. Regular Members as prescribed by article II (A) shall only be eligible to tender candidacy for the executive committee and cast vote.

2. The EC shall have the right to fix the date and venue of GA meeting as deemed necessary by the agenda and the participation of the members. This information should be issued by the executive committee at least before 1 month of the program going to be held. The information can be announced in the home page of SONSIK as well as on electronic media.

3. Meetings of the General Assembly shall be as following:

(a) Annual General Assembly: This meeting shall be convened once in a year by the Executive Committee.

(b) Special General Assembly: Two third majorities of the regular members may demand for special general assembly along with their name and signature.

4. Notice to all the regular members shall compulsory be served at least 21 days prior to the Meeting for Annual General Assembly and at least 7 days prior to special General Assembly specifying the venue, date, and time along with agenda of discussion.

5. Participation in person or through any form of electronic media shall be valid for the purpose of any meeting.

6. All the Members of SONSIK shall be informed about the meeting and the agenda by the EC through the common mail and/or official webpage and/or official social networking media of SONSIK.

7. (a) Participation of more than fifty percent of the regular member in the GA meeting shall be considered a valid quorum.

7. (b) If this quorum is not fulfilled within the stipulated duration, the EC may extend the duration as deemed necessary.

7. (c)After the extension of the duration, the GA shall be considered valid irrespective of the percentage of the participation.

8. Decision on any issue, raised in the general assembly or special general assembly except for those mentioned otherwise in this constitution, shall be made on the basis of simple majority of sound vote (ध्वनी मत) or casting vote by regular members.

B. Powers, duties, and functions of General Assembly

1. The Assembly shall take decision on fundamental policy guidance, plans, and programs as per rule of the society. Additionally, it shall provide essential approval to the Executive Committee decisions.

2. The Assembly shall elect the Executive Committee through secret ballet or on mutual consensus.

3. The Assembly shall review the activities of the Executive Committee and approve the financial and audit report after study and discussion presented by Secretary and Treasurer.

4. The Assembly shall approve amendment proposals of the Executive Committee on the Constitution, Rules and By Rules.

5. The Assembly shall approve the budget of long-term projects.

6. The Assembly may seek a clarification from any member regarding to have bad conduct or other wrong activity, in case appealed to the executive committee and may suspend, terminate or expel him/her from his/her membership in case the clarification submitted is found unsatisfactory.

C. Composition of the Executive Committee

1. There shall be an Executive Committee elected from the members of the General Assembly comprising a President, Five Vice Presidents, a Secretary, a Joint Secretary, a Treasurer, and four Members. Two members shall be selected by EC decision, which must include at least one student undergraduate level. Vice president shall be elected one from each of the following regions.

a. Gyeonggi-do, Seoul, Incheon, Gangwon-do (Self-Governing Province)

b. Chungcheongbuk-do (North Chungcheong), Chungcheongnam-do (South Chungcheong), Daejeon, Sejong

c. Jeollabuk-do (North Jeolla, Self-Governing Province), Jeollanam-do (South Jeolla), Jeonju, Gwangju

d. Gyeongsangbuk-do (North Gyeongsang), Gyeongsangnam-do (South Gyeongsang), Busan, Ulsan, Gyeongsan, Daegu, Andong

e. Jeju Special Self-Governing Province

2. In case, no member is present from any one of the above provinces assigned for the Vice President, the nomination will be carried out from the province, which contain highest number of the members present in the GA meeting. In case two provinces have the same number of members present, the final nomination will be decided by the approval of election committee.

3. All the positions of the Executive Committee shall be elected or selected from the General Assembly through Election or Consensus procedure.

4. The tenure of the Executive Committee shall be of one year from the date of appointment an election of the Executive Committee shall be held in one year. In case of unavoidable circumstances such as pandemics, natural disasters etc.; the existing committee shall continue its functions maximum for 6 more months with the close coordination of advisors and affiliated members.

5. Meeting of the Executive Committee shall be called at least 6 times in a year by Secretary as directed by President. But in case 51 percent out of total members of the Executive Committee demand to call a meeting, President shall give direction to Secretary to call a meeting. In the Absence of President, the meeting may continue with 51 percent of Executive Committee chaired by a vice-president directed by the president or senior vice president by age present in the meeting.

6. Meeting of the Executive Committee shall be held in the presence of at least 51 percent members out of total members of the Executive Committee.

7. In the meeting of Executive Committee, majority decision shall be valid and in case of tie of vote, President may cast decisive vote.

8. Each member of the Executive Committee shall be accountable towards the decisions of Executive Committee.

9. If the post of Executive Committee other than president becomes vacant, it could be appointed by Executive Committee decision for the remaining tenure.

10. All the activities of the Society shall be effective from the Executive Committee decision.

D. Powers, Duties, and Functions of the Executive Committee

1. To prepare yearly program and budget estimate of the Society for the next fiscal year.

2. To implement decision of the General Assembly abiding by the provisions of the constitution.

3. To represent the Society officially.

4. To perform essential work establishing relationship with relevant organizations, foundations, governments, and individual supporters to protect and promote profession interest and privilege of the Society.

5. To carry out daily administrative tasks in accordance with Constitution and Rules of th Society.

6. To present draft annual program and budget before the General Assembly for approval.

7. To award membership based on the norms set by the constitution.

8. To submit explanation in the General Assembly, in case if any action has been taken or intend to take as per the decision of general assembly or special general assembly against any EC member.

9. To take responsibility of the daily activities related to account and administration and operate financial transaction pursuant to the fiscal rules.

10. May call emergency General Assembly as per need.

11. Shall appoint its team of representatives to represent the Society to participate in the local, national, and international conference, seminar, meeting or tour.8

12. Any Member of the Executive Committee may be suspended or terminate from the post by a 2/3rd majority vote of all EC members of the Society. Such suspension or removal may be made on any of the following grounds (a) Inefficiency (b) Abuse of the Office (c) Disloyalty towards the Society (d) Gross misconduct (e) Immorality (f) Other grounds deemed actionable by the GA.

13. The EC holds the right to form different sub-committee to carry out specific tasks as and when necessary. The EC shall announce the mandate and the tenure of such a sub-committee at the time of its formation. The tenure of the sub-committee will not be more than the tenure of the Executive Committee.

14. The EC should report to the GA.

E. Regional undergraduate students wing

1. The Executive Committee may establish undergraduate wings in each province to enhance student engagement and regional coordination. These wings shall be organized according to the regions mentioned in section C (1).

2. Each regional undergraduate wing shall operate independently but remain accountable to the corresponding Regional Vice President.

3. Wings may raise funds independently or charge a membership fee, subject to the EC’s guidelines and approval.

4. Regional wings shall have seven members in total. One person shall be appointed as the coordinator who is responsible for leading activities and managing administrative matters.5. The Head shall oversee any funds raised through events or membership fees and is required to return any remaining funds to SONSIK before the next GA.

6. The undergraduate wings must be established within one (1) month of the GA.

7. Each wing shall submit an annual activity plan during its first official meeting, outlining events to be held until the next GA. This plan must be reported to the respective Regional Vice President.

Article IV: Candidacy, Powers, Duties, and Functions of the Office Bearers

A. Candidacy

1. All executive members of Executive Committee shall be elected through the election procedure as specified in this constitution.

2. All executive members of Executive Committee candidates must be Regular member of SONSIK.

3. For the candidacy of the President, the regular member must have served at least a year already in SONSIK as executive member of EC and should not hold any vital post of political organizations or its wings at the time of candidacy or term.

4. For the candidacy of executive members other than President, article IV (A.3) will not be applicable.

B. Duties and responsibilities of the executive members

1. President

1.1. The President shall be the Chief Executive of the Society and shall execute the provision of this constitution.

1.2. The President shall serve as the Chairperson of every meeting and the Annual General Meeting of the Society.

1.3. The President shall be responsible for calling meetings and creating agendas.

1.4. The President shall bear the ultimate responsibilities for the integrity and efficiency of the Society.

1.5. If the position of the President falls vacant for whatever reasons, the EC shall immediately nominate an acting President from among the existing Vice Presidents.

1.6 The President shall have the authority to dissolve or reorganize any regional undergraduate wing as established under Article-III, Section-E, based on performance, compliance, or alignment with the Society’s objectives.

1.7 The President, together with secretary and treasurer shall be responsible for maintaining SONSIK’s official website and ensuring that its contents are accurate, up to date, and reflective of the Society’s current activities and announcements.

2. Vice president

2.1. The Vice President shall assist the President in the fulfilment of all his/her duties.

2.2. The Vice President shall be responsible for coordinating SONSIK events, meetings, campaigns and many impromptu events that may arise under approval and supervision of the President.

2.3. The Vice-Presidents of respective regions shall plan, execute and take responsibility for the program and events in their respective regions.

2.4. In absence of President, The Vice-President shall assume the duties of the President according to article III (C-5).

2.5. Regional Vice Presidents shall participate in the formation of undergraduate wings and kid’s clubs in their respective regions, as outlined in Section “D (1), and E” shall oversee thei activities to ensure alignment with the goals and regulations of the Society.

3. Secretary

3.1. The Secretary shall conduct the meetings and programs of the society.

3.2. The Secretary shall prepare agendas of executive committee meetings with the consultation of President and shall notify it with the date and venue of EC meeting to all EC members.

3.3. The Secretary shall keep minutes, records, letter head, password of electronic record and data as well as other properties that belong to the SONSIK.

3.4. The Secretary shall attend all the meetings, prepare the business thereof and keep record of all proceedings including the full handling of SONSIK website.

3.5. The Secretary shall be in close contact with all SONSIK members and disseminate information about the activities of SONSIK to the members via email and/or official SONSIK webpage and/or official SONSIK social network media.

4. Joint Secretary

4.1. The Joint Secretary shall assist the Secretary in the fulfilment of all his / her duties.

4.2. The Joint Secretary shall coordinate and take responsibility of SONSIK Publication works.

4.3. In absence of Secretary, the Joint Secretary will discharge the responsibilities of Secretary.

5. Treasurer

5.1. The Treasurer shall lead the Society for financial discipline and transparency.

5.2. The Treasurer shall keep the account of all assets, liabilities, receipts, and disbursements of SONSIK.

5.3. The Treasurer shall collect membership fees, dues, and payment.

5.4. The Treasurer shall present a detailed Annual Financial Report of SONSIK during the Annual General Meeting.

5.5. The Treasurer shall prepare and present the financial plan for the tenure in coordination with President and other executive committee members.

5.6. In absence of Treasurer, with the approval of a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the EC members shall immediately nominate an acting treasurer from existing EC or regular members.

5.7. Acting Treasurer shall assume the duties of the Treasurer in the event of absence, dismissal or resignation until the post is filled by the EC.

6. Executive Member

6.1. The Executive Member shall attend the meetings regularly and actively participate on discussions.

6.2. The Executive Member shall stay informed about the Society’s mission, services, policies, and programs.

6.3. The Executive Member shall be responsible for proper supervision of the activities carried out by Subcommittees formed by the EC.

6.4. The Executive Members shall perform designated tasks and bear responsibilities given by the decision of the Executive Committee.

C. Vacancies and Removal of the EC Member

1. The post shall be considered vacant if any of the following condition applies.

(a) If he/she fails to attain three consecutive official EC meetings (due to genuine reason) without prior notification to EC. The genuineness of his/her reason will be as per the decision of other EC members.

(b) If a two-third (2/3) majority of the EC Members deem him/her to be incapable or unfit to respond to the assigned duty.

(c) If he/she is deceased.

(d) If he/she ceases to be the Regular member of SONSIK.

(e) If he/she resigned from his post. Such changed should be informed to all SONSIK members from SONSIK official webpage or some other electronic media.

2. Any executive member post shall be considered vacant under conditions in Article IV (C.1. a-e).

D. Election of executive committee

1. Executive committee (President, Vice-presidents, Secretary, Joint-Secretary, Treasurer, and members) shall be elected through direct voting by Regular members at the GA meeting held at the end of the tenure of the existing EC.

2. The EC shall make nomination(s) to fill vacant positions, if any from the Regular members (except for president) or from inside the EC. Duration of such nominee shall not outlast the term of the EC.

3. In case any of the following situations arises, an election shall be held at the earliest at the proposal of EC.

(a) If the posts of the President and all the Vice Presidents become vacant at once due to resignation or any other causes.

(b) If more than 2/3rd posts of the EC including those of the President and the Secretary become vacant at once due to unforeseen reasons.

Article V: Financial Arrangements

A. Financial Sources

1. There shall be a separate fund of the society with the deposits of the following amount received to this society.

2. Membership fees from the Regular and affiliated members of SONSIK.

3. Amount received as donation and assistance. Such donation, fund or any kind of assistance to the SONSIK should be approved from EC prior to receive it.

4. Executive Committee may determine other sources of income.

5. Amount received through fund raising programs organized by SONSIK.

B. Account Management

1. Amounts received from any sources on the name of this organization shall be deposited to the SONSIK official bank account.

2. The account shall be operated from joint signatures of President or Secretary and Treasurer of Society.

3. The Society shall arrange to spend and maintain the records of expenditures according to prevailing acts and rules.

C. Expenditure of the fund

1. The fund shall be handled by the Executive Committee for the functioning of society, carrying out its programs, publication of Society’s bulletin and other regular activities of society as approved by EC.

2. Utilization of fund for any purpose other than the article V (C.1) shall be approved in advance by the GA.

3. Financial transaction shall be made through banking system if possible. But transactions up to KRW 100,000.000 shall be arranged to be operated on cash by treasurer, or other as specified by Executive Committee.

4. All the procedures of procurement to payment of the society shall be under the financial rules and budget. In relation to budget that must be specified by executive committee.

5. In case of emergency, President and treasurer may spend up to KRW 100000 at a time. Such expense shall have to be approved from the forthcoming Executive committee meeting with 2/3rd majority.

6. In case of expenses not included in the fiscal estimated budget plan as specified by executive committee, approval from the forthcoming executive committee meeting is required.

7. The Treasurer shall keep records of all the incomes and expenditures, and an Annua Financial Report should be presented in the General Assembly meeting.

D. Student Contingency Fund and Management

1. SONSIK shall help Nepalese students studying in Korea through Student Contingency Fund.

2. Under no circumstances this fund shall be used other than health and educational purposes.

3. The final decision on beneficiary according to article V (D.1) shall be taken by EC.

4. Within the defined areas mentioned in article V (D.2), the specific case shall be decided by EC.

i. The source of fund shall be managed through direct donation in the name of this fund. ii. 15% on the saved money of each program organized during fiscal year. iii. Other means as decided by executive committee.

E. Audit

1. The account of income and expenditure of organization shall be maintained according to prevailing laws.

2. The auditing of the society shall be performed by the auditor as appointed by executive committee.

3. The Executive Committee shall nominate any regular/affiliated member(s) of Society, who is/are currently not holding any Executive committee member position, as the Auditor(s) to audit the accounts of Society before presenting the Annual Financial Report to the GA.

4. If the local authority wants to inspect or makes arrangements inspection of the account of the society deputing subordinate employee, it may do it at any time.

5. The program coordination committee shall disclose the total income and expenditure of each program at the end of the respective event through social audit.

Article VI: Provisions Related to Election

A. Provision Related to Election

1. For the election of Executive Committee, an election committee shall be constituted not exceeding 3 members by General Assembly. Such election committee shall automatically be dissolved after completion of such election.

2. Procedures of election shall be specified by election committee.3. Only one person can be elected from one university for the post of President, Vice Presidents and Secretary. Further only one person can be elected as executive committee member from same university.

4. One person may be re-elected for not more than 2 terms in any post of President, Vice president, Secretary, Joint-Secretary, and Treasurer in the executive committee.

5. The voting system shall be decided by the Election committee.

6. The candidate receiving the highest number of votes for each post shall be declared elected.

7. The decision of the Election Committee shall be considered final if any dispute arises in the process of election.

8. The Election Committee shall conduct the oath of the elected executive member collectively or individually according to the format set by the Election committee itself.

B. Duties and responsibilities of election committee

1. Make necessary arrangement for the election of the EC.

2. Follow all the basic procedure for the election and declaration of the election result.

3. Submit the final result to the GA for its approval.

Article VII: SONSIK Publications

A. SONSIK e-Newsletter and Souvenir

1. The society shall publish SONSIK e-Newsletter and SONSIK Souvenir.

2. SONSIK e-Newsletter shall be published at least two volumes in a year, and the souvenir shall be published annually.

3. An e-copy shall be made available to all members of Society via official webpage and/o social networking media.

B. Editorial Board

1. The EC shall nominate the Editor in Chief and other members of the Editorial Board for the publications.

2. The EC shall also nominate one of its office bearers to coordinate between the Editorial Board and the EC.

Article VIII: Advisory Committee

A. Advisory committee

1. EC shall form an Advisory Committee within 30 days from the start of its tenure.

2. EC shall recommend any Advisory Committee member if and when felt necessary.

3. Such a committee shall act as an advisory body to SONSIK and shall consist of a Chairperson and other members as deemed necessary by the EC.

4. The EC may recommend for nomination of any person who is directly or indirectly related to SONSIK or Nepal, who is a well-wisher of SONSIK, and whose inclusion would be beneficial to SONSIK, as the Chairperson or Member of the Advisory Committee.

5. The President of SONSIK shall act as the Member Secretary and the immediate past President of SONSIK shall be the ex officio member of the Advisory Committee.

6. The tenure of the Advisory Committee will be same as the executive committee.

Article IX: Miscellaneous

A. No Confidence Motion

1. No confidence motion shall have to be registered duly in writing in the society specifying name of the office bearer(s) along with following procedures:

(a) Must be in decent English or Nepali Language.

(b) Subject matter must be clear and practical.

(c) Must be with the signature of one fourth of the members of the general assembly along with their names and address.

2. To discuss on the no confidence motion registered under article IX (A.1), notice shall be served to the members and office bearers or the general assembly or special general assembly pursuant to this constitution along with the time, date, and place of such assembly.

3. Procedures of Discussion over the No Confidence Motion:

(a) Chairman of current advisory committee shall give permission to present the motion before the assembly pursuant to sub section 1, and a member amongst the proponents shall views along with reasons of the motion before the assembly.

(b) After the presentation of the member in favour of the motion pursuant to sub section 3(a), the Chairman of current advisory committee may give permission to maximum of 3 member to present their views.

(c) Chairman of current advisory committee may give permission to the office bearers against whom the motion is presented to present their defence.

4. Decision on the No Confidence Motion:

(a) Chairman of current advisory committee shall table the motion to decide on the majority in favour and declare the results adopting any procedure out of the following: (i) By separating members in two groups one in favour and another against, or (ii) By secret ballet.

(b) No confident motion shall be adopted by two third majorities of the members of the assembly. On such a condition adoption of motion to remove from the post, such members shall be expelled.

B. Objection Submission Procedure

1. Any member of SONSIK can submit the objection.

2. Identification of submitter must be enclosed in the objection application.

3. The objection submitted through official email of SONSIK shall only be considered as valid.

4. The EC shall respond to such objection within one week.

C. Formulation of Rules and Policies

1. The Executive Committee may formulate and enforce essential rules, policies, and procedures to smoothly discharge the functions of Society.

2. ‘Special Decisions’ under the framework of article I (D. 1-5) may be made at any regularly called meetings of the Executive Committee to deal with any unforeseen conditions of emergencies.

3. ‘Special Decisions’ shall be ratified by a simple majority vote in a General Assembly meeting within a period of three months or following annual General Assembly meeting.

D. SONSIK Alumni

1. Nepalese individuals graduated from Korean Universities shall considered as SONSIK Alumni.

2. SONSIK will keep an active network with its alumni to work for mutual interests and benefit.

E. Employee Recruitment

To operate daily activities, plans and programs of the society, post of employees, salary, allowances and other facilities and terms and conditions of employees, procedure to be adopted shall be as specified by Executive committee.

F. Amendment of the constitution

In case of necessity to make amendment on the Constitution, amendment motion shall be presented in the General Assembly and shall have to be adopted by two third majority of the General Assembly.

G. Affectivity of the constitution

This constitution shall be effective as soon as it is approved by the GA meeting.

H. Dissolution of the organization

1. General Assembly may dissolve the society through passage of a special resolution as I deem essential.

2. After cleaning all the liabilities of the society, all the property left shall be given to Nepal government.

I. Interpretation

The executive committee shall have a right to give final interpretation of the rules, procedures, and plans formulated under the constitution. But the final interpretation of constitution shall be given by General Assembly.

J. Secrecy to be maintained

No official or member shall act against this society. No official or the executive committee or members of General Assembly shall use uncivilized and vulgar words. The society maintains secrecy of the secret matters. If anyone fails to do that, action shall be taken as per the executive committee decision.

K. To be as per the laws

Contents not stated in this constitution shall be held in accordance with the prevailing laws, and contents inconsistent with prevailing Acts, Rules, and Laws shall be held void to the extent of such inconsistency.

Annex I: Logo

The SONSIK logo prominently features symbolic elements that represent unity and cooperation between Nepal and Republic of Korea. It is enclosed within a blue, gear-like circular frame, with the text "Society of Nepalese Students in Korea" along the top and "SONSIK" at the bottom, reflecting the organization's identity. At the centre, a white map outline of Nepal signifies the origin of the student members. Flanking the map are the national flags of Nepal (left) and Republic of Korea (right), placed side-by-side to symbolize bilateral connection and cultural exchange. A handshake in the centre represents friendship, solidarity, and partnership between Nepalese students and the Korean academic and social Accepted community. Beneath the map, the text “SONSIK 2004” marks the founding year of the society.

Annex II: SONSIK stamp

The official SONSIK stamp features the acronym “SONSIK” in bold red letters within a square border.
