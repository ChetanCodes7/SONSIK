23rd Executive Committee Expands Emergency Fund

Published on March 1, 2026 | Category: Executive Welfare

The Executive Committee of SONSIK met to finalize new medical discount protocols and hospital partner extensions for Nepalese students throughout Korea.

Under President Sonam Sherpa's leadership, the committee established enhanced welfare grants for active members needing emergency relief.
