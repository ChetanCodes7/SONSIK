About SONSIK

Institutional History & Mission

Founded in 2004, the Society of Nepalese Students in Korea (SONSIK) is the non-profit, non-political student organization.

It is a unified and established organization representing Nepalese students across the Republic of Korea, working to promote academic, professional, cultural, and social cooperation among Nepali students and to connect them with educational institutions and relevant organizations in Korea and Nepal. Founded in 2004, SONSIK has been serving the Nepalese student community in Korea for more than two decades, with a constitution, elected Executive Committee, regional representation, and established organizational procedures. SONSIK provides students with a platform to connect with fellow Nepalese students, share reliable information and opportunities, participate in academic and cultural activities, receive support during difficulties, and contribute to the welfare of the wider Nepalese student community. As an organization with a long-standing institutional presence and recognition/registration with the relevant authorities, SONSIK aims to provide a trusted, organized, and responsible platform for Nepalese students throughout Korea. Joining SONSIK means becoming part of a wider student network where you can connect, learn, participate, support one another, and grow together while studying in Korea.
